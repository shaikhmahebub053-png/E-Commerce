    Popular Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How can I track my order?

    Use the "Track My Order" option above or visit your Orders page to see real-time updates.

    How can I cancel an order?

    If your order hasn't shipped you may cancel from the orders page or request cancellation here.

    How do I return a product?

    Open the order, select the item and choose "Return" to start the returns flow.

    When will I receive my refund?

    Refunds are processed within 3-7 business days after approval, depending on your bank.

    What payment methods are supported?

    We accept major credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI and selected wallets.

    What should I do if my payment failed?

    Check your card details and retry, or contact your bank. You can also use another payment method.

    How can I change my delivery address?

    Update addresses in Account settings or during checkout before placing your order.

    How can I contact customer support?

    Use our Live Chat, Email Support, or Call Support options below to reach our team.
